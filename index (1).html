The Estate

Built for gathering, designed for memory-making

Spring Lake Manor sits on a sheltered cove of Spring Lake — a beautifully quiet body of water with minimal boat traffic, clear skies, and true serenity. The 9,000-square-foot estate was purpose-built for meaningful, unhurried connections.

Step outside onto 2.5 private acres of pristine forest, a rare shallow-entry sandy beach, and a private swimming dock. Inside, soaring windows frame the wilderness, flooding grand living spaces with warm, natural light.

Getting Here

720 South Shore Drive, South River, Ontario

By Car from Toronto

Take Highway 400 north to Highway 11 north. Exit at 282 (Boundary Rd / Mountainview Rd). Head west to Tower Road and follow signs to Eagle Lake Rd. Continue past Eagle Lake Narrows Country Store for about 10 minutes. Turn left on South Shore Drive and continue to 720 at the end of the road.

Drive Time

Approximately 3 hours from the Greater Toronto Area via Highway 400 and Highway 11.

Nearest Towns

Sundridge and South River are about 25 minutes away for groceries, LCBO, and essentials. North Bay (1 hour north) and Huntsville (1 hour south) have full dining and entertainment.

Guest Policies

No pets. No smoking or vaping indoors. Outdoor quiet hours 10 PM – 8 AM. Minimum booking age 25.
