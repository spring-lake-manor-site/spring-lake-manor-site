Corporate Retreats

One private estate, not a scattered block of hotel rooms

Most retreat venues put your team in separate hotel rooms and a rented meeting room down the hall — no shared space to actually unwind together between sessions. Spring Lake Manor is a single 9,000 sq ft private estate: everyone sleeps, eats, meets, and decompresses under one roof, on 2.5 private acres on Spring Lake, three hours north of Toronto.

Edwin Hall — a 2,000 sq ft lounge with a fireplace and cathedral ceilings — works equally well as a workshop space, a presentation room, or the spot where the team actually talks after the agenda ends. Starlink Wi-Fi keeps hybrid sessions and follow-up emails running, and the chef's kitchen means catered meals or a team cooking session are both on the table.
